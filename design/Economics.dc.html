How holding is checked

  1. Connect and sign a message. No transaction, no approval.
  2. We read your $TRENCH balance, including tokens staked on-site.
  3. Tier uses your lowest balance over the last 72h, so flash-buying before a purchase doesn't count.
  4. Re-checked at checkout; one tier per wallet, linked wallets don't stack.
