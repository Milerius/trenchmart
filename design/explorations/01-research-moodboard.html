What good crypto-marketplace UX looks like in 2026

TrenchMart design · phase 1
Wallet connectOne action: connect + sign inUse Wallet Standard discovery and solana:signIn (SIWS) so connect and sign happen in one click, with signMessage as fallback. On Android, MWA blocks a programmatic second popup. Show discovery as a loading state, not "no wallets". Say plainly that it is a message, not a transaction.solana.com/docs/frontend/messages-and-auth · docs.solanamobile.com ux-guidelines
Before signingShow the exact money movementList every line before the wallet opens: price, fee, discount, total, destination (escrow vault), plus a network-fee estimate. Wallets simulate too, so our numbers must match theirs to the lamport. Re-quote if a quote is stale and show its age.MetaMask simulations · Tenderly tx preview · ScamSniffer spoofing report
Tx status4 states, not a spinneridle → awaiting signature ("Approve in your wallet") → pending (sig + explorer link) → confirmed / failed. On Solana, "confirmed" (~0.6s) is the success state; "finalized" (~13s) runs in the background before payout. A wallet rejection is a cancel, not an error: no red.Solana cookbook confirmation · Helius commitment levels · cryptoskills frontend-ux
EscrowA visible timeline with an ownerPaid → in escrow (countdown) → released or disputed → refunded. Every step shows who acts next and when. Put protection copy right next to the pay button, not in an FAQ. "Processing" with no time frame causes anxiety.martech.zone trust-by-design · anoda marketplace UX · u1core patterns
Trust signalsSpecific over genericStars from everyone sit at 4.7–4.9 and mean little. Lead with checkable facts: which checks passed, the pinned sha256, bond staked, disputes lost, verified-buyer reviews, sales count. Every security claim must match a check that actually ran.u1core · anoda · TrenchMart docs/01 principles
Errors & emptyHuman copy, a next actionMap wallet and RPC errors to plain text: "Not enough SOL: you need 1.53 + ~0.000005 network fee, you have 0.9". Blockhash expired means "Quote expired, get a new one". Empty states offer a next step. Skeletons match the final layout so nothing jumps.MLH 100 days of Solana · OnchainKit error-handling · MiniPay best practices
