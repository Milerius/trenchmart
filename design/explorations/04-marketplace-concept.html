Decisions: cards show what you pay once your tier is known, and the sidebar says how that number is built. Filters use list price so results don't reshuffle when your tier changes. Trust filters (verified only, bond, no outbound, no wallet permissions) come straight from the security report fields. Empty and error states always offer a next action, and the error says what is not affected.

Owner question: should "In AI review" listings be visible to buyers at all (greyed out, not buyable), or hidden until live? This concept hides them by default behind the "AI-verified only" filter, which is on.
