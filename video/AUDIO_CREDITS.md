## How the audio is made

- **Music:** an original 100 BPM track in F minor, rendered sample-by-sample at 48 kHz by a small
  JavaScript DSP library (`audio/dsp.ts`: polyBLEP oscillators, biquad filters, a Freeverb-style
  reverb and a ping-pong delay). Every section change lands on a scene cut from `src/timeline.ts`, so
  the music and the picture share one clock.
- **SFX:** every click, keystroke, scan tick, coin, lock and chime is synthesized and placed on the
  frame of its on-screen event (`audio/sfx.ts` reads the same timeline constants as the scenes).
- **Voiceover:** the eight lines in `audio/vo-lines.json` are spoken by Kokoro-82M (voice
  `af_heart`, 1.05x) through `audio/tts.py`. In the full mix the music is ducked about 7 dB under
  the voice.
- **Mastering:** `audio/build.ts` measures loudness with ffmpeg's EBU R128 filter and adjusts gain,
  then applies a true-peak-safe limiter. Both mixes land at -14 LUFS integrated with a -1 dBTP
  ceiling, and both renders encode them as 192 kbps AAC.

## Launch videos

The four launch videos use the same instruments and processing:

- **Music:** `audio/launch/music.ts` renders a separate 100 BPM track for each video, with its own
  key and chord progression (`audio/launch/<id>.ts`). Fills and crashes land on that video's scene
  cuts.
- **SFX:** each video's cues are placed on the frames of its on-screen events, read from
  `src/launch/<id>/timeline.ts`.
- **Voiceover:** Kokoro-82M `af_heart` at 1.05x reads `src/launch/<id>/vo.json`, ducking the music
  about 7 dB.
- **Captions:** faster-whisper runs on the generated voiceover at build time to time each caption
  word. It produces no audio.
- **Mastering:** `audio/launch/build.ts` masters every mix to -14 LUFS integrated with a -1 dBTP
  ceiling, and writes it to `public/audio/launch/<id>.flac`.
